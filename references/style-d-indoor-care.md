# DOG_STYLE_D_INDOOR_CARE_KEYFRAME

## Purpose

Use for clear indoor pet-care instruction in a Japanese 2D light-realistic style. It prioritizes usable evidence of feeding, grooming, cleaning, training, rest, or health-support actions. This is optional and not the current default M2-D style.

## Visual language

- clean Japanese 2D linework with mild realism;
- neutral domestic palette and soft indoor daylight;
- accurate household and pet-care props;
- uncluttered medium or medium-close framing;
- restrained expressions and calm instructional tone;
- clear hand-object-dog relationships.

## Action evidence

Each applicable cell must visibly answer: who performs the care action, which tool/object is used, where it is applied, and what completion/result is visible. Keep the large action whole. Use extra phase cells only when setup and result are both necessary to understand the method.

## Locks

- stable room and prop positions across a continuous scene;
- correct container openings, tool orientation, contact surface, and dog anatomy;
- factual content remains within approved evidence and veterinary/safety limits.

## Prohibitions

- no generic posed portrait in place of the care action;
- no text, subtitles, labels, numbers, logos, watermark, or UI;
- no medical claim or technique not approved in the blueprint;
- no body-object penetration, floating tools, duplicated hands, closed-door passage, or impossible occlusion;
- no dramatic fantasy lighting, glossy 3D, or comic exaggeration that obscures the method.
