# DOG_STYLE_C_GHIBLI_PET_NARRATIVE

## Purpose

Use for warm Japanese 2D lifestyle storytelling centered on a dog's situation and emotion inside a believable living environment. This is an optional style module; it is not the current default M2-D test style.

## Visual language

- hand-drawn Japanese 2D animation feeling with warm natural light;
- soft but controlled colors, textured domestic surfaces, and lived-in environmental detail;
- expressive yet natural dog posture and face;
- cinematic medium-wide compositions that let the environment tell part of the story;
- gentle emotional transitions: uncertainty, curiosity, trust, comfort, or companionship;
- human figures remain supportive and physically plausible.

## Scene priorities

Show where the dog is, how it relates to the room and person, what large action is happening, and what emotional result follows. Use windows, doors, bedding, feeding/care objects, and light direction as narrative context, not decoration.

## Locks

- consistent dog breed/markings, owner appearance, room layout, prop identity, and time-of-day within one continuous scene;
- clear physical paths and usable apertures for doors/crates/barriers;
- no unexplained scene teleportation or prop movement.

## Prohibitions

- no direct imitation of a specific copyrighted film frame or named living artist;
- no text, subtitles, numbers, logos, watermark, UI, or panel labels;
- no glossy 3D, photorealistic rendering, chibi distortion, neon effects, or hyper-saturated game art;
- no body-object penetration or physically impossible action.
