# DOG_STYLE_E_REACTION_RESONANCE

## Purpose

Use for Japanese 2D, lightly anthropomorphic reaction and resonance scenes built around contrast, recognition, or family-life emotion. It is optional and not the current default M2-D style.

## Visual language

- clean Japanese 2D illustration with slightly exaggerated but readable reactions;
- strong visual contrast between before/after, expectation/reality, or dog/human response;
- simple domestic settings and clear body language;
- expressive faces and ears/tail posture without turning the dog into a human body;
- balanced humor, warmth, and recognition rather than slapstick chaos.

## Composition

Use paired or sequential reaction beats only when they correspond to real scene/narrative functions. A 2x2 layout is available but never mandatory. The R6 scene-plan count determines 2x2, 3x3, 4x4, or 5x5.

## Locks

- preserve character, dog, setting, prop, and color continuity;
- make the triggering event and resulting reaction causally legible;
- keep the dog anatomically canine even when expression is slightly anthropomorphic.

## Prohibitions

- no invented reaction that changes the approved meaning;
- no decorative reaction panel used as filler;
- no text, subtitles, speech bubbles, labels, numbers, logos, watermark, UI, or emojis;
- no grotesque faces, human hands on the dog, extra limbs, body-object penetration, or impossible scene transitions;
- no glossy 3D, photorealism, or hyper-saturated game/anime effects unless a custom style explicitly replaces this module.
